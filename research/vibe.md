- **Interior walls & ceiling (36, 28, 49):** warm yellow `#f2c872` fading to cream `#f6e6a1` / `#fcf9e8`, white stucco cornices with gold trim, crystal chandeliers.
- **Wood & wine wall (28, 01, 53):** walnut/mahogany shelving packed with bottles `#722b1b`, `#825135`, lit highlights `#a8906a`.
